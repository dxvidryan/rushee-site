Rushée Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

Rushée ("we", "us") is an app that helps people prepare for sorority recruitment. This policy explains what information Rushée collects, why, who processes it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. Rushée is operated by [legal name]. Contact: [contact email].

Who can use Rushée

Rushée is for people aged 16 and older. The first screen asks for your birth month and year. If you are under 16, the app stops and collects nothing else. If you are 16 or older, we keep only your age band (16–17 or 18+). We never store your full date of birth.

Kept only on your device: chapter notes, your recs and legacy tracker, round checklists, full round reflections, and the Home Screen widget's countdown. They are encrypted on your device, are not sent to our servers, and are removed when you delete your account in the app.

Notifications are scheduled on your device. Their text contains no personal information from your plan.

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, camera, health data, or browsing history. We do not sell your information, share it for targeted advertising, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

AI coaching

Rushée's coach is an AI. Before your first AI session we ask whether you want to turn AI coaching on. If you say no, planning, schedules and notes still work. You can turn AI coaching off at any time under You → Privacy & Data.

When AI coaching is on, the following is sent to our AI providers to produce a response: your voice or typed words during a session, your Practice answers (to write your report), selected plan details such as your main goal, and your latest "next time" reflection focus if you wrote one. Inputs and outputs are checked by automated safety systems. If a message suggests you may be in danger, Rushée stops ordinary coaching and shows US crisis resources (988 and 911). Rushée is not emergency support, and no person is alerted.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety, or as part of a sale or reorganization of the business, in which case this policy continues to apply.

Your choices and rights

In the app you can:

  • Export your data: You → Privacy & Data → Export my data.
  • Delete a Practice Session and its transcript and report.
  • Turn AI coaching off, which stops new AI sessions.
  • Delete your account: You → Privacy & Data → Delete account. We delete your account data from our live systems immediately and revoke Rushée's access to your Sign in with Apple account. Copies in encrypted backups are overwritten within 30 days. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription; cancel it in your Apple account settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your information, or to object to some processing. Contact [contact email] and we will respond as the law requires.

Security

Information travels over encrypted connections. Account data is encrypted in our database, and on-device data is protected by iOS encryption. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit who can access information and keep it only as long as described above.

Changes

We will update this policy when our practices change and show the new date at the top. For significant changes we will tell you in the app.
