Common questions

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone? Open Rushée and tap Restore Purchases on the plan screen or under You → Access. Use the same Apple account you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription? In Rushée, go to You → Manage subscription, or open your Apple account settings → Subscriptions. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

What is Coach Time? The minutes of live voice coaching included in your plan: 180 a month on Monthly, 45 a week on Weekly. Included minutes reset each period. Extra minutes you buy never expire. Quick questions by text don't use Coach Time.

The coach can't hear me. Check that Rushée has microphone access in iPhone Settings → Rushée. You can also type your answers in any session.

How do I turn AI coaching off? Go to You → Privacy & Data and switch off AI coaching. Planning, schedules and notes still work.

How do I get a copy of my data? Go to You → Privacy & Data → Export my data.

How do I delete my account? Go to You → Privacy & Data → Delete account. This removes your plan, reports, notes and schedule, and forfeits unused Coach Time. It does not cancel an Apple subscription, so cancel that first if you no longer want it.

Is Rushée connected to my university or a sorority? No. Rushée is independent preparation software. Always follow your campus's official recruitment information.
